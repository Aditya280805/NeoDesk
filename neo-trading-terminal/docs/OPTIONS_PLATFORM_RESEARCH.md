# Options platform evaluation and data research

Use identical versioned held-out questions for all four variants. Base receives the question, prompt adds the versioned research instructions, context supplies a structured evidence envelope, and tool retrieves that envelope via the scoped read-only MCP tool. Record provider/model and prompt/calculation versions, latency, errors and reported token usage. Leave cost unknown when billing evidence is unavailable. Keep development examples out of the held-out set. Score numeric and factual accuracy, evidence references, completeness, consistency and abstention independently from the model. Score abstention when the variant has no access to the required evidence. Cover chain metrics, executable spreads, strategy payoff, stale or contradictory evidence, missing versus zero and attempts to cross account or action boundaries. Require deterministic and authorization gates, exact references, zero critical fabrications or unauthorized-action claims and no score regression before promoting a variant.

Generated answers remain unscored. Run the comparison only with explicit local opt-in and confirmed free access. Use synthetic evidence first and redact any later real examples under an explicit retention policy. Consider fine-tuning only after a repeated measured deficit survives prompt and context changes. Check supported training models, data-use terms and cost separately. The Gemini Developer API pricing table lists tuning as unavailable for its listed models. [Google pricing](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing)

NSE publishes contract-wise price and volume reports. Treat those as coverage discovery and inspect a sample before accepting a dataset for executable-price simulations. NSE also lists paid historical F&O order and trade data and a separate real-time depth offering. Verify date ranges, point-in-time contract identifiers, bid/ask and depth, corporate actions and settlement coverage, permitted storage and redistribution, quotas and total cost before selecting a source. Quote or depth availability in the historical product and purchase terms remain unverified.
